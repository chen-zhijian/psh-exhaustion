5 November 2026

Title
Regularity of plurisubharmonic exhaustions and related problems
Authors: Zhijian Chen and Bo Xiao

Scope
The author requested a revised introduction, clearer terminology and
notation, explicit historical references, and removal of redundant scope
qualifications. The same editorial approach was then applied throughout
the paper. The author subsequently requested numbered equivalence
statements in Theorem 3.4 and Proposition 5.3, an explicit identification
of Theorem 6.1 with Theorem 1.4, the shorter title, and publication as
version 4 in the research repository and on the personal website.

Introduction
The introduction defines continuous psh exhaustions before introducing
weak 1-completeness and the smoothness question. Weak pseudoconvexity is
used for the smooth condition. Demailly's broader definition is explained
with references to his thesis and L2 paper. The complete Kähler conclusion
is stated for Kähler manifolds. A Main Results subsection precedes the
theorems, regularity conventions are explained in place, and the five
applications have numbered, bold titles. The threefold theorems use
C^{0,1}_{loc} and C^{1,0}. The Hironaka construction, surface problem,
logarithmic collar, and comparison with Ohsawa receive connecting text
and explicit references.

Sections 2–6
The revision adds short explanations of the proof strategy and unifies
notation. It clarifies the ruled-surface cocycle and first-normal
derivative argument, global metric construction, averaging and radius
recovery, modification invariance, exceptional-curve calculation,
surface boundary cases, and the curve-fibering argument. Theorem 3.4
uses two numbered equivalent conditions. Proposition 5.3 uses three,
with the metric criterion supplying the degree obstruction directly.
Theorem 6.1 is restated as (=Theorem 1.4), and the surface theorem in
Section 5 is connected explicitly to Theorem 1.3. The local convexity
argument retains the continuous exhaustion that controls vertical
escape. Redundant negative-scope remarks have been removed.
