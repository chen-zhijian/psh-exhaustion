Scope: original psh-exhaustion-revised/main.tex, lines 2366-2695, with

Conclusion: no fatal gap found in the theorem for reduced pure-dimensional
complex spaces with continuous real-valued psh exhaustion and a holomorphic
map to C^(n-1) having fibers of dimension at most one. The source's explicit
dimension and continuity hypotheses must remain. The extension to arbitrary
singularities of the total space is supported by the cycle argument.

Primary-source checks
1. Barlet--Magnusson (1998), Chapter I, section 1(b), printed p.816,
   explicitly gives geometric flatness for equidimensional maps over a normal
   base, with no properness requirement. The pure one-dimensional fiber
   condition follows branchwise from the analytic dimension theorem.
   https://www.numdam.org/item/ASENS_1998_4_31_6_811_0.pdf
2. Barlet--Magnusson, On nearly smooth complex spaces, Definition 1.1.1 and
   Remark (ii), author-preprint pp.3-4, independently confirms the same
   equivalence and points to the book, Chapter IV, Theorem 3.4.1.
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/1710.08105
3. Compact-support integration continuity is cited to Barlet--Magnusson
   (2014), Chapter IV, Proposition 2.3.1. Barlet (2017), Theorem 4.0.7,
   proof Step 4, author-preprint p.21, explicitly confirms this location.
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/1707.07962
4. Barlet (1999), Theorem 1, printed p.26, explicitly states Bishop
   compactness for any complex analytic space, with fixed compact support
   and bounded Hermitian volume. Its support-continuity statement also
   verifies the limit argument retaining the marked point x.
   https://library.slmath.org/books/Book37/files/barlet.pdf
5. The complete locally supplied Forum Math. 2022 article (Vijiitu2022.pdf;
   extracted text tmp/forum2022/Vijiitu2022-full.txt) was checked at printed
   pp.456-457 and 463-464. Propositions 2.2 and 2.4 and Corollary 2.5 apply
   to reduced complex spaces. Corollary 4.2 is only needed for the known
   smooth-source argument; the replacement stability lemma removes this
   restriction.

Proof checks and clarifications retained
- Effective geometric fiber multiplicities, not scheme-theoretic flatness,
  supply the uniform area bound for reduced component cycles.
- The maximum principle forces compact curves into one fixed compact
  sublevel before Bishop compactness is applied. No global Kaehler form is
  required, and the curves may lie wholly in the singular locus.
- Compact support plus local finiteness makes the central fiber's compact-
  component exhaustion values locally finite; this provides the collar gap.
- A compact curve analytic in the open collar is closed and analytic in X,
  so it is a full component of a pure one-dimensional fiber. The collar's
  reduced fibers thus contain no compact irreducible components and are Stein.
- The auxiliary smooth function in the printed proof of Forum Corollary 2.5
  is not itself an exhaustion of the open domain. The supplied draft already
  repaired this omission by adding its continuous psh exhaustion. The rewrite
  preserves that necessary repair and explicitly uses boundedness away from
  the collar to establish 1-convexity.
- The Remmert pullback gives a new smooth psh exhaustion, not smooth
  approximation of the prescribed rho or strict pshness on X. Normality of X
  and of its Remmert reduction are not claimed.
- Nontrivial Remmert fibers are finite connected unions of compact fiber
  curves; the continuous exhaustion rules out infinite connected chains.
