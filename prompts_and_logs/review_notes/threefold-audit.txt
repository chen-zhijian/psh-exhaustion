Review of the threefold construction (October 29, 2026)

Scope: original revised manuscript, sections 2--4, lines 401--1218.
Result: no mathematical gap was found in the stated threefold theorems or
their proofs. The replacement fragment retains all essential calculations.
The changes here are consolidation and proof clarification, not repairs of
a disproved theorem. This is a mathematical reading, not formal verification.

Checks performed

1. Geometry and attribution. The six branch points give genus two. Serre
duality gives H^1(C,K_C)=C; all nonzero extensions have equivalent middle
terms after scalar changes. The negative section has normal bundle K_C^-1,
and L=O(Y) tensor pi^*K_C is trivial on Y, nef and big with L^2=2.
The construction agrees with Koike, Example 4.1; it is not presented as a
new ruled-surface example.

2. C^2 obstruction. The frame and fiber-coordinate transition signs were
checked. Positivity plus the zero tangential Levi entry forces the mixed
entry to vanish. The first normal derivative is C^1 holomorphic; its
transition law makes the given extension split. Only second derivatives
are used. The claim excludes C^2 semipositive metrics, not C^1 metrics.

3. Cusp endpoint. In scaled vectors (xi,w nu), the leading Levi form of
q^a is uniformly positive definite and of order |w|^(2a). The initial
metric contributes O(|w|). Consequently a large coefficient works at
a=1/2; shrinking alone is sufficient only below the endpoint. The
Lipschitz conclusion and removable-singularity step are valid.

4. Global metric. The logarithmic branch has curvature pi^*Theta(K_C)
off Y. Its strict domination on a collar of q=epsilon validates the
piecewise maximum. The cusp dominates near Y. The finite positive metric
and its local Lipschitz regularity therefore follow. Minimal singularities
follow from the bounded global potential and compactness. Zhang's question
explicitly includes the modified example; the original Grauert bundle is
not identified or claimed solved.

5. Radius criterion. Compact-group averaging preserves proper sublevels.
Convexity in log squared radius and a level above the zero section give
a unique radius with strictly positive radial derivative. The implicit
Levi calculation includes the factors 1/2 from Re(zeta). The negative
radius logarithms transform as metric weights with the correct sign.
This excludes every C^2 exhaustion, independently of the constructed one.

6. Compact modifications. Truncating above the compact exceptional
neighborhood avoids unsupported smooth descent of an arbitrary psh
function. Properness gives compact sublevels in both directions.

7. Hironaka construction. The line and conic meet transversely twice.
Opposite-order blowups glue over the complement of these points.
Hausdorffness and properness are local over this base cover. Exceptional
surfaces are compact even for noncompact X; their rational fiber divisors
give ordinary homology relations. The local surface blowup verifies
multiplicity one. The sum of two positive rational curves is null
homologous, so a Kahler form cannot exist. This agrees with Barlet section
6 and Stacks Tag 08J1; the compact support over one point comes from placing
the configuration in the exceptional plane of Bl_x(X).
